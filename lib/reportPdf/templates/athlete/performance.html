Performance Interpretation
Load absorption

Session distance climbed through the block (7,800 → 9,100 m) before easing to 8,600 m, with max velocity peaking at 8.6 m/s on the highest-volume session — output rising with load rather than falling away from it. That combination indicates load is being absorbed, not accumulated.

Asymmetry trend — monitor

Nordic curl asymmetry has risen across three consecutive tests (3.1% → 4.0% → 9.2%), right-dominant. Still below the 15% threshold, but the direction is consistent and the most recent jump is the largest. This coincided with the highest-volume week of the block, which is the most likely explanation — and the one worth testing by re-measuring after a lighter week.

Nutrition implications

Two things follow from this load pattern: protein distribution matters more during high-volume weeks than total protein does, and the post-session window is where your adherence has been weakest (61% supplement compliance). Both are addressed in your current Nutrition prescription. No change to energy targets is indicated by these numbers alone.

Injury risk assessment: Low-Moderate

No single metric crosses a threshold. The combination of a rising asymmetry trend and the block's highest training volume moves this from Low to Low-Moderate — monitored rather than actioned, with a re-test scheduled rather than a load reduction imposed.

Recommendations
1

Share the Nordic curl trend with the strength coach before the next heavy block — proactive, not urgent.

2

Re-test asymmetry after a lower-volume week to separate genuine imbalance from fatigue-driven variation.

3

No GPS load modification indicated — current distances sit within the expected range for this block and output is holding.

Monitoring Plan
Next measurement

GPS is captured every session automatically. Force-plate testing repeats weekly on the same device and protocol, ideally at a consistent point in the weekly cycle — testing on a Monday after a rest day and a Friday after three sessions will not produce comparable numbers.
